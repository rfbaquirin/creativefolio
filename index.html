Brand Strategy · Identity · Crisis Communications

German European School of Manila — Brand Partner

Not a one-off project — a brand relationship that had to hold up through a pandemic.

Brand Strategy Visual Identity Crisis Communications Content Strategy Copywriting

Brief

GESM needed a brand strategy and identity system built from the ground up, under a formal Marketing Consultancy Agreement, one built to carry through the website and ongoing social media. That mandate shifted hard when the pandemic suspended face-to-face classes: parents comparing home-based online learning to the in-person experience worried they weren't getting value for their money, that their children weren't being taught properly, on top of now supervising school from home themselves.

Strategy

Built the foundational brand strategy and visual identity system first. When the pandemic hit, reframed the communication around one idea: learning never stops. Anchored it in three pillars, continuous quality education through real and virtual classrooms, an international curriculum (IB, Cambridge, and German curriculum) tied to global citizenship standards, and a nurturing, values-driven community, built around parent personas shaped by the specific anxieties families were actually facing.

Execution

Carried the identity through GESM's website and social channels, then ran a full-funnel 2020 content strategy, created and curated content, Facebook ads, and landing pages, moving parents from awareness to enrollment. Real executions included a "Why Choose GESM?" infographic video built for lead generation and enrollment announcements carrying hashtags like #LearningInTheNewNormal and #FutureThatMatters.
